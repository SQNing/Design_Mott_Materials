Research task C: a new cubic exchange model

Use a simple cubic lattice with primitive vectors (1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1)
and one magnetic site at fractional position (0,0,0). The structural target is
the full cubic point group, all 48 signed permutation matrices, with no
fractional translation.

There is only isotropic nearest-neighbour exchange. Its coefficient is -0.93
in dimensionless model energy units. Count each unoriented bond once: use the
positive x, y and z translations. No onsite or higher-body terms are present.

I want a classical reference in a 2 by 2 by 2 periodic cell, then the harmonic
spectrum at q=(0,0,0) and q=(0.5,0,0) in primitive reciprocal coordinates.

The physical operator normalization still needs clarification: should the
operators be ordinary spin-1/2 operators or unit-length Pauli operators?
